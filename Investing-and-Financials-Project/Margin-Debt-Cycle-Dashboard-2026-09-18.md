## Market-cycle assessment

Classification: Late-cycle speculative expansion, with a Distribution-watch overlay.

Why this label: Prices remain near cycle highs, margin debt is historically large and still growing on a year-over-year basis, valuations (CAPE and Buffett) are in extreme long-horizon territory, and leadership is narrow. That is the signature of late-cycle speculative expansion. The overlay is distribution watch, not confirmed distribution: internals are weak, small caps lag, midweek volatility appeared and then vanished, and July showed that leverage can reverse quickly. Confirmed deleveraging is not the right label while August debt rose and indexes held the 7600 area.

What would change the classification:

- To confirmed deleveraging: a second consecutive FINRA monthly decline in margin debt coinciding with a break in major indexes, widening HY OAS through 4–5%, and a VIX regime that stays above ~20 with term-structure backwardation.
- To distribution watch as the primary label: failed retests of the August high, persistent negative breadth, and equity-financing/SOFR stress that stops looking one-day/technical.
- Back toward markup / expansion: broadening breadth, a cooling in YoY margin-debt growth without a price crash, and less crowded mega-cap/AI leadership.

Concise interpretation: The market looks resilient on the surface and fragile underneath. It is not actively deteriorating this Friday — credit is quiet, VIX closed calm, and the cap-weighted index held. The pressure gauges that matter for cycle risk — margin leverage, concentration, and valuation — remain elevated. These are pressure gauges, not precise market-timing clocks.
