## Market-cycle assessment

Classification: Late-cycle speculative expansion, with a Distribution-watch overlay.

Why this label: Prices remain near cycle highs while margin debt is historically large and still growing on a year-over-year basis, valuations (CAPE and Buffett) are in extreme long-horizon territory, and leadership is still concentrated in Nasdaq, semiconductors, and mega-cap technology. That is the signature of late-cycle speculative expansion. The overlay is distribution watch, not confirmed distribution: the S&P and Dow fell on the week, Nasdaq new lows outnumbered new highs, and high-yield spreads have started to leak wider, but Friday was an up day, the S&P is still within about 1% of its record, and the last FINRA print rose. Confirmed deleveraging is not the right label.

What would change the classification:

- To confirmed deleveraging: a second consecutive FINRA monthly decline in margin debt coinciding with a break in major indexes, HY OAS through 4–5%, and a VIX regime that stays above about 20 with term-structure backwardation.
- To distribution watch as the primary label: a failed retest that takes the S&P decisively off the highs, persistent negative breadth, and equity-financing or SOFR stress that stops looking technical — or a continued HY/CCC widening that starts to pull investment-grade spreads with it.
- Back toward markup / expansion: broadening breadth that includes the Russell 2000 and equal-weight on a sustained basis, a cooling in year-over-year margin-debt growth without a price crash, and less crowded mega-cap/AI leadership.

Concise interpretation: The market looks resilient on the surface and fragile underneath. It is not actively deteriorating — VIX closed calm in contango, investment-grade spreads are tight, and Friday’s session rallied — but high-yield and CCC spreads are no longer fully quiet, and the pressure gauges that matter for cycle risk (margin leverage, concentration, and valuation) remain elevated. These are pressure gauges, not precise market-timing clocks.
